# How to ask so Jarvis (or Friday) opens his window

The words below open the window by fixed rules, whatever the model thinks; the same works with Friday.

## A problem with numbers: worked step by step

Say "solve this" (or "step by step", "work it out", "walk me through", "meka solve karanna", "visandanna",
"විසඳන්න"), then the whole question with its numbers, and its parts (a), (b) if it has them:

> Solve this step by step: a car starts from rest and accelerates at 2.5 m/s² for 8 s, then brakes at
> 6 m/s² until it stops. (a) Its top speed? (b) The braking distance?

It needs at least two numbers and a full question. Numbers are best typed or pasted (Whisper can write
them as words, in Sinhala most of all), or use **Solve from a photo or PDF**. Then: "part b", "hint",
"next step", "back", "full solution", "I got 33.3", "practice", "summary" (or "b kotasa pennanna",
"hint ekak denna", "ilaga piyawara", "mulu uththaraya pennanna").

## An idea: a visual lesson

Say **visualise**, **animate**, **simulate**, **visually**, **in 3D**, or **show me in the window**:

> Visualise how a transistor amplifies a signal. Animate a wave on a string. Simulate a pendulum.
> Explain entropy visually. Entropy eka window eke pennanna. Fourier series visualize karala pennanna.
> වින්ඩෝ එකේ පෙන්නන්න. ඇනිමේෂන් එකක් එක්ක තරංග පෙන්නන්න.

After he explains something in words, "show me" or "pennanna" turns that explanation into a lesson, and
"yes" accepts his offer ("Want me to show it?"). During it: "next", "back", "pause", "again".

## MATLAB or Octave

Name it and say what to do: "**in MATLAB**, solve y'' + y = 0 and plot it", "simulate a damped spring in
Octave". He writes the code on the board; it runs when you click Run (or say yes).

## Formulas and graphs on the board

"Put the working on the board", "plot sin x and cos x on the board": the model does these, so it may
sometimes answer in words. "Visualise ..." always opens a lesson instead.

These words don't open a lesson: the map, the weather, news and videos (they have their own tools), and
"take apart" or "inside" with a car, bike or engine (the 3D explorer).
